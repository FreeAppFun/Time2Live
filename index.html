Vitality & Risk Breakdown

Demographic Baseline: Built upon period life table data published by national statistical agencies (such as UK ONS, France INSEE, and US CDC/SSA).

Survivorship Principle: As you achieve higher ages, expected lifespan naturally expands because earlier life hazards have already been navigated.

Statistical Anomalies & Skews: Actuarial probabilities can occasionally throw up unusual results due to confounding factors. For example, total alcohol abstainers sometimes exhibit slightly skewed negative mortality rates compared to moderate drinkers—a phenomenon largely driven by "sick quitters" (individuals who stopped drinking entirely due to pre-existing serious medical conditions).
